Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Where is La Bonita Brunch?

We're a food truck at 958 NW 6th St in Redmond, Oregon, just north of Brightside Thrift.

What are your hours?

We're open Monday through Friday from 7:00 am to 2:30 pm and Saturday from 7:00 am to 2:00 pm. We're closed on Sunday. Online ordering for pickup is open until 2:00 pm on weekdays and 1:30 pm on Saturday.

What kind of food do you serve?

We serve homemade Mexican breakfast and brunch: breakfast burritos, burritos, tacos, flautas, churro bites and carlota.

Can I order online?

Yes. You can order ahead for pickup through our Clover online ordering page.

Do you deliver?

Yes. You can get delivery through DoorDash. For pickup, order ahead on our Clover page.

Do you speak Spanish?

Sí. ¡Hablamos español! Our family speaks English and Spanish at the window.

Can I get my burrito as a bowl?

Yes. Any burrito can be made as a bowl with no tortilla.

Do you have meatless options?

Yes. The Ranchero, Nopal, Miles and Bean & Cheese burritos, the Mexican Scramble and Day's Tacos have no meat.

Do you offer anything for veterans?

Yes. Coffee is free for veterans. Just let us know at the window.

Is there seating?

Yes. We have tables and umbrellas next to the truck where you can eat or wait for your order.

Are your eggs fresh?

Yes. We crack real eggs for every order. We never use powdered or pre-packaged eggs.
