# Real Eggs, Cracked to Order: How We Make Our Breakfast Burritos

![Fresh eggs cracked on the griddle with pico de gallo at La Bonita Brunch in Redmond, OR](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/6a99a8c673bc423a39f07352/6af0fa555c24a80d1bd2b1e0/6af0fc315c24a80d1bd2de29/1794178642797/la-bonita-fresh-eggs-griddle.jpg)

At La Bonita Brunch, there's one rule we don't bend: every egg is a real egg, cracked when you order. We never use powdered or pre-packaged eggs. It takes a little longer, and we think you can taste the difference.

## Why fresh-cracked eggs matter

Eggs cooked to order stay tender and full of flavor. Whether they're scrambled into a Breakfast Burrito or left runny in a Ranchero, they hit the griddle when your order comes in, not hours earlier.

## Our breakfast burritos

- **Ranchero Burrito:** runny eggs, beans, cheese, green salsa and sour cream.
- **Breakfast Burrito:** eggs, potato and cheese with your choice of meat, like bacon.
- **Mia's Burrito:** egg, potato and cheese.
- **Breakfast Asada Burrito:** a breakfast burrito made with carne asada.
- **Nopal Burrito:** eggs, beans, cheese and grilled nopales (cactus).
- **Miles' Burrito:** eggs, beans and cheese. A simple classic.

## Can I get it as a bowl?

Yes. Any burrito can be made as a bowl with no tortilla. Just ask when you order.

## Do you have meatless breakfast options?

Yes. The Ranchero, Nopal, Miles', Mia's and Bean & Cheese burritos, Day's Tacos and the Bean N Cheese Taco are all meatless.

## Add our house salsas

Our green salsa and habanero salsa are made in house. Ask for a side of either, or both, to go with your burrito.

## What else we make by hand

Fresh eggs are only part of it. Our green salsa and habanero salsa are made in house, guacamole is made fresh every day, and every order is cooked when you place it. If you're in a hurry, order ahead online and it'll be ready at the window.

## Build a better breakfast

Add a side to make it your own: guacamole made fresh every day, crema, beans or fries. Or turn any burrito into a bowl with no tortilla. Want something on the side? Our Asada Breakfast Tacos (3) come with carne asada, guacamole, onion and cilantro, and the Chicken Breakfast is a full plate with eggs, cheese and potato.
