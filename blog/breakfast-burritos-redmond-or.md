# Breakfast Burritos in Redmond, OR: Every One on Our Menu, by Style

Breakfast burritos are why most people find our food truck at 958 NW 6th St. Here's every one on the menu, grouped by how you like your breakfast, so you can pick fast at the window or online.

## Meatless and egg-forward

- **Ranchero Burrito:** runny eggs, beans, cheese, green salsa and sour cream. Our fan favorite.
- **Miles' Burrito:** eggs, beans and cheese.
- **Mia's Burrito:** egg, potato and cheese.
- **Nopal Burrito:** eggs, beans, cheese and nopales (cactus).

## With meat

- **Breakfast Burrito:** your choice of meat, like bacon, with eggs, potato and cheese.
- **Breakfast Asada Burrito:** carne asada, eggs, cheese and potato in a flour tortilla.
- **Huevos & Winnies Burrito:** grilled winnies, eggs, potato and cheese. New on the menu.

## Not in a burrito mood?

- **Breakfast Tacos (3):** three corn tortillas filled with your choice of protein, eggs, cheese and potatoes.
- **Asada Breakfast Tacos (3):** three corn tortillas with carne asada, guacamole, onion and cilantro.
- **Chicken Breakfast:** a chicken plate with eggs, cheese and potato.

## Make it a bowl

Any burrito can be made as a bowl with no tortilla. Just ask when you order.

## Add a salsa

Our green salsa and habanero salsa are made in house. Add a side of either, plus guacamole or crema.

## Why they taste different

Every egg is cracked fresh when you order. We never use powdered or pre-packaged eggs. [Here's how we make them](https://labonitabrunch.net/blog/real-eggs-cracked-to-order).

## How to pick yours

- **Want it saucy?** Get the Ranchero. The runny eggs and green salsa soak into everything.
- **Keeping it simple?** Miles' (eggs, beans and cheese) and Mia's (egg, potato and cheese) are the classics.
- **Need it to last until dinner?** The Breakfast Asada Burrito or a Breakfast Burrito with bacon.
- **Something different?** The Nopal Burrito brings tender cactus, and the Huevos & Winnies Burrito is pure comfort.

## What to drink with it

Pair your burrito with a café de olla, our Mexican spiced coffee (free for veterans), or something cold: Mexican Coca-Cola in the glass bottle, a Jarritos, orange or apple juice or sugar-free green iced tea.

## When to come

We're open Monday through Friday 7am to 2:30pm and Saturday 7am to 2pm. Online orders are taken until 2:00pm on weekdays and 1:30pm on Saturday, and delivery is on DoorDash.

## Breakfast burrito questions

**What time can I get a breakfast burrito?**

We open at 7:00am Monday through Saturday and stay open until 2:30pm on weekdays and 2:00pm on Saturday. We're closed on Sunday.

**Which breakfast burritos have no meat?**

The Ranchero, Miles', Mia's and Nopal burritos are all meatless.

**Can I get a breakfast burrito without a tortilla?**

Yes. Ask for any burrito as a bowl.

**Can I order ahead?**

Yes. Order online for pickup until 2:00pm on weekdays and 1:30pm on Saturday, then grab it at the window.

**Do you deliver?**

Yes, through DoorDash.
