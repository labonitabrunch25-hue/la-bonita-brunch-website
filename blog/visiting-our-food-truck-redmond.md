# Visiting Our Food Truck on NW 6th St in Redmond: What to Know

Here's everything you need for your first trip to La Bonita Brunch, the family-owned Mexican breakfast and brunch food truck in Redmond.

## Where we are

We're at **958 NW 6th St, Redmond, OR 97756**, just north of Brightside Thrift. [Get directions](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=958+NW+6th+St+Redmond+OR+97756).

## When we're open

- **Monday–Friday:** 7:00 am – 2:30 pm
- **Saturday:** 7:00 am – 2:00 pm
- **Sunday:** Closed

## How to order

- **Walk up** to the window and order.
- **Order ahead online** for pickup. Online orders are taken until 2:00pm on weekdays and 1:30pm on Saturday.
- **Get delivery** through DoorDash.
- **Call us** at (541) 678-4991.

## Where to sit

We have tables and umbrellas next to the truck, so you can eat here or wait for your order. Pets are welcome at the tables.

## Good to know

- We're family-owned, women-owned and Latino-owned. ¡Hablamos español!
- Veterans get free coffee. Just let us know at the window.
- Any burrito can be made as a bowl, and we have plenty of meatless options.
- Every egg is cracked fresh when you order.
