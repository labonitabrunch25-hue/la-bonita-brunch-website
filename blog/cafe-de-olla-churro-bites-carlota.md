# Café de Olla, Churro Bites and Carlota: Our Mexican Coffee and Sweets

![Churro bites with cinnamon sugar from La Bonita Brunch in Redmond, OR](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/6a99a8c673bc423a39f07352/6af0fa555c24a80d1bd2b1e0/6af0fcbc02b24b2679cb1680/1794178609057/la-bonita-churro-bites.jpg)

Breakfast is our specialty, but some of our favorite things come at the end of the meal. Here's a quick look at the coffee and sweets you'll find at our truck.

## What is café de olla?

Café de olla is a traditional Mexican spiced coffee. It's named for the clay pot, or olla, it's traditionally brewed in, and it's often made with cinnamon and piloncillo, an unrefined cane sugar. Ours is warm, comforting and perfect with a breakfast burrito.

## Free coffee for veterans

If you've served, your coffee is on us. Just let us know at the window. It's our small way of saying thank you.

## Churro bites

Our churro bites are coated in cinnamon sugar and finished with a drizzle of cajeta, a rich Mexican caramel. They're easy to share, though you might not want to.

## Carlota

Carlota is a no-bake Mexican icebox cake. Ours has creamy layers of lemon cookies, topped with cajeta. If you're ordering online, you'll find it listed as Carlota Dessert.

## Something cold to drink

We keep Mexican Coca-Cola made with cane sugar and Jarritos fruit sodas ice cold in glass bottles, plus Red Bull, sugar-free green iced tea, orange or apple juice and bottled water.

## How to pair them

- **Café de olla and churro bites:** the classic. Dip a churro in the cajeta, then take a sip.
- **Carlota and a Jarritos:** the lemon cookie layers go great with a cold pineapple or mandarin Jarritos.
- **A breakfast burrito and café de olla:** our favorite way to start the morning.

## Make it a full meal

Coffee and dessert go best after a burrito. Start with a breakfast favorite like the Ranchero Burrito or the Breakfast Asada Burrito, or go lunch-style with the Broadway Burrito or Day's Tacos. Then finish sweet.
