# Mexican Lunch in Redmond: Burritos, Tacos and Flautas Until 2:30

Our name says brunch, but lunch is half the menu. We're open until 2:30pm on weekdays and 2pm on Saturday at 958 NW 6th St, and everything is made to order.

## Lunch burritos

- **Broadway Burrito:** carne asada, cheese, french fries, guacamole and sour cream. The big one.
- **Asada Burrito:** carne asada, beans, cheese, tomato, onion, cilantro and guacamole.
- **Chicken Burrito:** chicken, beans, cheese, tomato, onion, cilantro, lettuce and sour cream.
- **Fish Burrito:** battered fish, cabbage, pico and special sauce.
- **Shrimp Burrito:** grilled shrimp, cabbage, pico and special sauce. Great as a bowl.
- **Chile Relleno Burrito:** battered roasted pepper filled with cheese, with beans and sour cream.
- **Bean & Cheese Burrito:** beans and cheese. Simple and perfect.

## Tacos and flautas

- **Day's Tacos (4):** four crispy potato tacos topped with lettuce, cheese, tomato and sour cream.
- **Las Flautas:** rolled taquitos with your choice of beef or chicken, topped with sour cream, guacamole, cheese and lettuce.
- **Asada Taco:** carne asada, onion, cilantro and guacamole on a corn tortilla.
- **Shrimp Taco** and **Fish Taco:** cabbage, pico and our special sauce.
- **Chicken Taco:** chicken, onion, cilantro and lettuce.
- **Taco Chile Relleno (2):** two corn tortillas with chile relleno, cheese, beans and sour cream.

## Something sweet, something cold

Finish with churro bites with cajeta or a slice of carlota, our no-bake Mexican icebox cake. To drink: Mexican Coca-Cola, Jarritos (pineapple, lime, mandarin, fruit punch or tamarind) or café de olla.

## Which lunch is right for you?

- **Hungriest person in the group:** the Broadway Burrito. Carne asada and french fries inside the same tortilla.
- **Lighter lunch:** a couple of Asada or Chicken Tacos, or a Shrimp Taco and a Fish Taco.
- **Meatless:** the Bean & Cheese Burrito, Day's Tacos or the Bean N Cheese Taco.
- **Sharing:** a plate of Las Flautas or Day's Tacos for the table.

## Lunch questions

**How late do you serve lunch?**

Until 2:30pm Monday through Friday and 2:00pm on Saturday. We're closed on Sunday.

**Can I get my lunch burrito as a bowl?**

Yes. Any burrito can be made as a bowl with no tortilla. The Shrimp Burrito is especially good that way.

**Do you have meatless lunch options?**

Yes. The Bean & Cheese Burrito, Day's Tacos and the Bean N Cheese Taco have no meat.

**What salsas do you have?**

Our green salsa and habanero salsa are made in house. Ask for a side of either, plus guacamole or crema.

**Do you deliver lunch?**

Yes. We deliver through DoorDash in the Redmond area.

**Do you speak Spanish?**

Sí. ¡Hablamos español!
