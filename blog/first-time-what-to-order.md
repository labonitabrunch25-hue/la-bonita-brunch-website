# First Time at La Bonita Brunch? Here's What to Order

![Breakfast burrito with café de olla and Mexican sodas at La Bonita Brunch food truck in Redmond, OR](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/6a99a8c673bc423a39f07352/6af0fa555c24a80d1bd2b1e0/6af0fab2f1031b7f41ae3ed2/1794178564325/la-bonita-breakfast-burrito.jpg)

If you've driven past our truck at 958 NW 6th St in Redmond and wondered what to get, this guide is for you. We're a family-owned Mexican breakfast and brunch food truck, and everything on the menu is made fresh to order.

## Start with the Ranchero Burrito

Ask our regulars and most will point you to the Ranchero Burrito. It's runny eggs, beans, cheese, our house green salsa and sour cream, all wrapped up hot off the griddle. It's simple, it's filling, and it's meatless.

## Want meat? Try a Breakfast Burrito or the Broadway

Our Breakfast Burrito comes with eggs, potato and cheese plus your choice of meat, like bacon. If you're more of a lunch person, the Broadway Burrito is a big one: carne asada, cheese, french fries, guacamole and sour cream.

## Don't skip the tacos and flautas

Day's Tacos are four crispy potato tacos topped with lettuce, cheese, tomato and sour cream. Our flautas are rolled taquitos with your choice of beef or chicken, topped with sour cream, guac, cheese and lettuce. Prefer something classic? The Asada Taco keeps it simple with carne asada, onion, cilantro and fresh guacamole.

## Any burrito can be a bowl

Skipping tortillas? Just ask. Any burrito on our menu can be made as a bowl.

## Save room for something sweet

Our churro bites come dusted in cinnamon sugar with a drizzle of cajeta, a Mexican caramel. Our carlota is a no-bake Mexican icebox cake that's cool, creamy and a little sweet-tart.

## How do I order?

Walk up to the window, or order ahead online for pickup so your food is ready when you get here. We're open Monday through Friday from 7am to 2:30pm and Saturday from 7am to 2pm. Grab a seat at our outdoor tables (pets welcome), and if you're a veteran, your coffee is on us.
