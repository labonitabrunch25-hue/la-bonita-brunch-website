# Our menu

Breakfast burritos, tacos & more. Every burrito can be made as a bowl. Current prices are in our [online ordering](https://la-bonita-brunch-redmond.cloveronline.com/menu/all).

## Breakfast

- **Ranchero Burrito** *(Fan favorite)*: Runny eggs, beans, cheese, green salsa and sour cream.
- **Breakfast Burrito** *(Fan favorite)*: Your choice of meat, like bacon, with eggs, potato and cheese. Pair it with an ice-cold drink, café de olla and a dessert.
- **Mexican Scramble**: Eggs scrambled with tomato, onion and cilantro, plus beans and cheese. Burrito or bowl.
- **Breakfast Asada Burrito**: A breakfast burrito with carne asada.
- **Nopal Burrito**: Eggs, beans, cheese and grilled nopales.
- **Miles Burrito**: Eggs, beans and cheese.
- **Chicken Breakfast**
- **Breakfast Tacos**
- **Asada Breakfast Taco**: A breakfast taco with carne asada.
- **Mia's Burrito**

## Burritos

- **Broadway Burrito** *(Big one)*: Steak, french fries, guacamole, sour cream and cheese.
- **Chicken Burrito**: Chicken, beans, cheese, onion, tomato, cilantro, lettuce and sour cream.
- **Fish Burrito**: Fish with cabbage, our special fish sauce, tomato, onion and cilantro.
- **Asada Burrito**: Carne asada, beans, guacamole, cheese, onion, cilantro and tomato. Try it with our green and habanero salsas.
- **Shrimp Burrito** *(Try it as a bowl)*: Grilled shrimp, cabbage, pico and special sauce.
- **Chile Relleno Burrito**: Battered roasted pepper stuffed with cheese, with beans and sour cream.
- **Bean & Cheese Burrito**: Beans and cheese. Simple and perfect.

## Tacos & Flautas

- **Day's Tacos** *(Fan favorite)*: Crispy fried tacos filled with mashed potato, topped with lettuce, tomato, sour cream and queso fresco.
- **Las Flautas**: Served in a cup over tomato broth, with lettuce, guac, sour cream, queso fresco and cheddar.
- **Asada Taco**: Carne asada, onion, cilantro and guacamole made fresh every day.
- **Shrimp Taco**: Shrimp with cabbage, tomato, onion, cilantro and our special shrimp sauce.
- **Chicken Taco**: Chicken, onion, cilantro and lettuce.
- **Fish Taco**: Fish, cabbage, pico and special sauce.
- **Taco Chile Relleno**: Our chile relleno, taco style.
- **Bean N Cheese Taco**: Beans and cheese in a taco.
- **Family Flautas**: A family-size order of our flautas.

## Sweets

- **Churro Bites**: Cinnamon sugar churros with a drizzle of cajeta, Mexican caramel.
- **Carlota**: A no-bake Mexican icebox cake. Cool, creamy and sweet-tart, topped with cajeta. Listed as Lemon Cookie Cream when you order.
- **Cookies**
