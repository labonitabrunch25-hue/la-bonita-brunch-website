# Our menu

*Live page: https://labonitabrunch.net/menu (also shown in the menu section of the homepage). Source: [source/menu-page.html](../source/menu-page.html).*

Breakfast burritos, tacos & more. Every burrito can be made as a bowl. Current prices are in our [online ordering](https://la-bonita-brunch-redmond.cloveronline.com/menu/all).

## Breakfast

- **Ranchero Burrito** *(Fan favorite)*: Runny eggs, beans, cheese, green salsa and sour cream.
- **Breakfast Burrito** *(Fan favorite)*: Your choice of meat, like bacon, with eggs, potato and cheese. Pair it with an ice-cold drink, café de olla and a dessert.
- **Breakfast Asada Burrito**: Carne asada, eggs, cheese and potato in a flour tortilla.
- **Huevos & Winnies Burrito**: Grilled winnies, eggs, potato and cheese. New.
- **Nopal Burrito**: Eggs, beans, cheese and nopales.
- **Miles' Burrito**: Eggs, beans and cheese.
- **Mia's Burrito**: Egg, potato and cheese.
- **Asada Breakfast Tacos (3)**: Three corn tortillas with carne asada, guacamole, onion and cilantro.
- **Chicken Breakfast**: A chicken plate with eggs, cheese and potato.
- **Breakfast Tacos (3)**: Three corn tortillas filled with your choice of protein, eggs, cheese and potatoes.

## Burritos

- **Broadway Burrito** *(Big one)*: Carne asada, cheese, french fries, guacamole and sour cream.
- **Chicken Burrito**: Chicken, beans, cheese, tomato, onion, cilantro, lettuce and sour cream.
- **Fish Burrito**: Battered fish, cabbage, pico and special sauce.
- **Asada Burrito**: Carne asada, beans, cheese, tomato, onion, cilantro and guacamole. Try it with our green and habanero salsas.
- **Shrimp Burrito** *(Try it as a bowl)*: Grilled shrimp, cabbage, pico and special sauce.
- **Chile Relleno Burrito**: Battered roasted pepper filled with cheese, with beans and sour cream.
- **Bean & Cheese Burrito**: Beans and cheese. Simple and perfect.

## Tacos & Flautas

- **Day's Tacos (4)** *(Fan favorite)*: Four crispy potato tacos topped with lettuce, cheese, tomato and sour cream.
- **Las Flautas**: Rolled taquitos with your choice of beef or chicken, topped with sour cream, guacamole, cheese and lettuce.
- **Asada Taco**: Carne asada, onion, cilantro and guacamole on a corn tortilla.
- **Shrimp Taco**: Grilled shrimp, cabbage, pico and special sauce.
- **Chicken Taco**: Chicken, onion, cilantro and lettuce.
- **Fish Taco**: Battered fish, cabbage, pico and special sauce.
- **Taco Chile Relleno (2)**: Two corn tortillas with chile relleno, cheese, beans and sour cream.
- **Bean N Cheese Taco**: Beans and cheese in a flour tortilla.

## Sweets

- **Churro Bites (8)**: Eight churro bites with cinnamon sugar and cajeta, Mexican caramel.
- **Carlota**: A no-bake Mexican icebox cake: creamy layers of lemon cookies topped with cajeta caramel.

- **Red Bull**: Ice cold.

- **Guacamole**: Made fresh every day.
- **Green Salsa**: Our house green salsa.

- **Crema**: Side of sour cream.
- **Beans**: Side of beans.
